# Enterprise Project Risk Management System

**Simulated project-management case study | €750,000 cloud migration**

This repository presents a risk and issue management simulation for a fictional €750,000 cloud-migration project. It demonstrates how risks are identified, assessed, prioritized, owned, mitigated, monitored and escalated throughout delivery.

## Business problem

The migration involves vendor dependency, data-privacy obligations, financial exposure, technical integration complexity and schedule pressure. Without structured risk governance, these factors could delay go-live, increase cost or create compliance and operational issues.

## Simulated role

Project Manager / PMO Analyst responsible for maintaining the risk register, facilitating risk reviews, assigning owners, monitoring mitigation actions and escalating materialized risks.

## Methodology

Qualitative and quantitative risk management, supported by a risk breakdown structure, probability-impact assessment, heatmaps, contingency reserves and issue escalation.

## Planned artifacts

- Risk management plan
- Risk breakdown structure
- Risk register with 25–30 risks
- Probability-impact matrix
- Risk heatmap
- Risk owners and response plans
- Contingency reserve analysis
- Issue log
- Monthly risk reports
- Escalation and corrective-action records

## Decision-making simulation

The case study will compare inherent and residual risk exposure, show a selected risk materializing into an issue, and document the escalation, corrective action, contingency use and governance decision.
