## Risk-process checklist

- [ ] Risk register maintained throughout delivery
- [ ] Every material risk has an owner and response plan
- [ ] Heatmap refreshed at agreed intervals
- [ ] Issues separated from risks
- [ ] Escalations documented with decisions
- [ ] Contingency use tracked and approved
- [ ] Residual risk reviewed before closure
