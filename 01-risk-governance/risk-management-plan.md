# Risk Management Plan — Cloud Migration

**Simulation scenario** | **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This plan defines how risks and issues will be identified, assessed, owned, mitigated, monitored and escalated during a simulated €750,000 cloud-migration project.

## 2. Risk management approach

- Maintain a central risk register throughout the project.
- Assess each risk using probability and impact.
- Assign a named owner and response strategy to every material risk.
- Review the top risks weekly and all risks at least monthly.
- Distinguish risks (potential future events) from issues (events that have occurred).
- Escalate high-impact or unresolved risks to the project sponsor and steering committee.

## 3. Risk scoring

| Score | Probability / Impact | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Very low | Rare or negligible effect |
| 2 | Low | Unlikely or minor effect |
| 3 | Medium | Possible or moderate effect |
| 4 | High | Likely or major effect |
| 5 | Very high | Almost certain or severe effect |

**Risk score = Probability x Impact**

| Risk score | Priority | Governance response |
|---|---|---|
| 15–25 | Critical | Immediate escalation and active mitigation |
| 8–14 | High | Owner-led mitigation and weekly monitoring |
| 4–6 | Medium | Monitor and manage within workstream |
| 1–3 | Low | Accept or monitor |

## 4. Risk categories

- Vendor and supplier
- Technical and integration
- Data privacy and security
- Financial and commercial
- Schedule and resource
- Operational and adoption
- Regulatory and compliance

## 5. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Project sponsor | Approves contingency use and major escalations |
| Project manager | Owns the risk process, facilitates reviews and reports exposure |
| Risk owner | Manages the assigned risk and mitigation actions |
| PMO analyst | Maintains the register, heatmap and reporting |
| Steering committee | Makes decisions on critical risks and reserve use |

## 6. Reporting

The monthly risk report will include the top ten risks, changes since the previous period, mitigation progress, emerging risks, issues, contingency status and decisions required.
